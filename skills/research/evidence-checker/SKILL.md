# Evidence Checker

## Purpose

Prevent factual or research claims from becoming stronger than the evidence supplied for them.

## When to use

Use when reviewing factual claims, research notes, technical statements, or literature summaries against supporting evidence.

## Instructions

1. Identify each important claim.
2. Locate supporting evidence in the supplied material.
3. Classify the claim as directly supported, partially supported, contradicted, or not established.
4. Explain the classification briefly.
5. Identify missing context or alternative interpretations.
6. Rewrite overly strong claims conservatively when requested.
7. Never fabricate sources, citations, results, statistics, or quotations.

## Inputs

- Draft claims or research notes
- Supporting papers or evidence
- Verification scope

## Outputs

- Claim-by-claim evidence assessment
- Missing or contradictory evidence
- Conservative corrections when requested

## Example

User: "Check whether this paragraph is supported by the cited paper."

Expected behavior: map each important claim to the supplied evidence and flag claims that go beyond it.

## Limitations

The checker cannot establish facts that are absent from the supplied evidence. Independent source verification may still be required.
