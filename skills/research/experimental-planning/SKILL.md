# Experimental Planning

## Purpose

Design a reproducible research experiment or study plan with explicit variables, controls, measurements, and validation criteria.

## When to use

Use when planning an experiment, study, benchmark, or other structured investigation.

## Instructions

1. Define the hypothesis and objectives.
2. Identify variables, controls, measurements, and sample selection.
3. Specify the procedure and analysis plan.
4. Define failure criteria and reproducibility requirements.
5. Separate exploratory steps from confirmatory tests.
6. Identify confounders and safety or ethics review requirements where applicable.
7. Do not fabricate expected results.

## Inputs

- Research question or hypothesis
- Available materials, data, or participants
- Constraints and measurement requirements

## Outputs

- Experimental plan
- Variables and controls
- Procedure and analysis plan
- Validation and failure criteria
- Reproducibility and risk considerations

## Example

User: "Design a benchmark to compare two algorithms."

Expected behavior: define datasets, controls, metrics, repeated trials, analysis procedure, and criteria for interpreting differences.

## Limitations

A plan cannot guarantee valid results. Real-world experiments may require domain expertise, safety review, ethics approval, or additional controls.
