# Physics Research Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze physics research claims using explicit assumptions, equations, measurements, and evidence.

## When to use

Use when evaluating experimental or theoretical physics papers, claims, simulations, or measurements.

## Instructions

1. Define the physical system and assumptions.
2. Extract equations, variables, units, and boundary conditions.
3. Check dimensional consistency.
4. Separate measured, simulated, derived, and claimed quantities.
5. Compare claims with the stated evidence.
6. Record uncertainty and limitations.
7. Identify additional evidence that could test the claim.
8. Never invent measurements or citations.

## Inputs

- Physics paper or supplied research text
- Equations, data, or measurements
- Specific claim or research question

## Outputs

- Structured physics analysis
- Assumption and equation review
- Evidence assessment
- Uncertainty and limitations
- Suggested verification steps

## Example

User: "Check whether the experimental data supports this theoretical claim."

Expected behavior: inspect assumptions, units, equations, and reported measurements before assessing the claim.

## Limitations

The analysis depends on the supplied model and evidence. Experimental validation and specialist review may still be required.
