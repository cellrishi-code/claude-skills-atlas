# Algorithms Learning

## Purpose

Teach algorithms through clear concepts, invariants, examples, complexity analysis, and progressively harder practice.

## When to use

Use when learning an algorithms or data-structures concept and the learner wants structured instruction.

## Instructions

1. State prerequisites.
2. Explain the core idea and invariant.
3. Walk through a small example.
4. Give time and space complexity.
5. Provide a worked example.
6. Give practice problems from easy to harder.
7. Ask the learner to attempt before revealing a full solution when requested.

## Inputs

- Algorithm or data-structure topic
- Learner level
- Specific problem or learning goal

## Outputs

- Concept explanation
- Example and complexity analysis
- Worked example
- Practice problems
- Verification or correction

## Example

User: "Teach me binary search and then give me practice problems."

Expected behavior: explain the invariant, trace a small example, analyze complexity, and provide progressively harder exercises.

## Limitations

Learning outcomes depend on practice and feedback. A worked example does not prove mastery of unseen problems.
